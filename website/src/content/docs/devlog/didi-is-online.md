This might be the start of an exciting journey, time will tell. Didi is live in the browser. It does some pixel art stuff, and some general digital painting stuff too: [try it](/app). The website version is free and will stay free. It is not open source yet, but it will be at some point.

## What is there

- **Two modes**, Pixel art and Painting, each with its own tools.
- **196 MyPaint brushes** for painting, with pen pressure and tilt.
- **Pixel art tools**: pixel brush with pixel-perfect lines, paint bucket, shapes, pixel and tile grids.
- **Layers, selections and transforms**, a command palette (Ctrl+K), dark and light themes.
- **Touch screens**: two-finger gestures, finger drawing.

## What comes next

Animation, then drawing lessons inside the app. How exciting is that? ❤️
