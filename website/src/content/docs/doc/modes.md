---
title: Pixel art and Painting
description: The two modes of a Didi document.
---

Each document has a mode, saved with it. The mode chooses the tools you see, their shortcuts and how the canvas is shown. The layout stays the same: switching modes moves nothing.

To switch the mode of the open document, click the layout menu in the top left corner, or open the command palette (**Ctrl+K**) and type "mode".

## Pixel art

This mode brings some quality-of-life features for pixel art.

- The tools are pixel-perfect (brush, eraser).
- From a certain zoom level, the zoom snaps to whole values, depending on the size of your pixels, to avoid weird distortions.
- The rotation is locked.

## Painting

Illustrations, concept art and textures, with soft brushes.

- The brushes come from the libmypaint library: airbrushes, watercolor, smudges…
- The zoom is free, and you can rotate the view.

## All tools stay available

A mode only chooses what is shown. You can easily switch between modes, and the tools of the other mode can still be picked from the command palette.
