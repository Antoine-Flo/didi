## Drawing tablets

Plug in your tablet and draw: Didi reads the pen's pressure and tilt. There is nothing else to set up.

## Mouse

Everything works with a mouse, without pressure. For pixel art, a mouse is perfectly fine.

## Touch screens

On a touch screen, like a tablet or a phone:

- **Two fingers** move and zoom the view. In Painting, they also rotate it.
- **Tap with two fingers** to undo, **with three fingers** to redo.
- **Drawing with a finger** is set in **Preferences → Tablet → Finger drawing**:
  - **Auto**: fingers draw until you use a pen, then they only move the view.
  - **On**: fingers always draw.
  - **Off**: fingers only move the view.
- While you draw with the pen, your palm is ignored.

On small screens, Didi switches to a compact layout. It can also be forced in the preferences.
